# financial-statement-analysis (S05)

**Used by:** `equities-analyst`, `real-estate-analyst`, `private-markets-analyst`, `governance-analyst`, `forensic-analyst`, `operating-businesses-analyst`
**Source docs:** `docs/04-skills-and-expert-roles_rev01.md`, `docs/11-management-and-ceo-profiling_rev00.md`

## Procedure
1. Get the filing from the primary source (SEC EDGAR, CVM/RAD, company IR) and note the period.
2. Revenue growth and mix; margins (gross, EBITDA, net); return on capital.
3. Cash conversion: operating cash flow vs. net income; capex; free cash flow.
4. Balance sheet: net debt/EBITDA, maturities, currency of debt (BRL vs. USD), covenants.
5. Earnings quality: accruals, one-offs, related-party items, auditor opinion and changes.
6. Read the notes on contingencies, leases and guarantees.

## Output
A one-page summary: key figures table (3–5 years), 3 strengths, 3 concerns, and open questions.
