# memo-writing (S20)

**Used by:** `orchestrator`, `chief-investment-officer`, `macro-economist`, `geopolitical-analyst`, `red-team`, `reporting-accountant`
**Source docs:** `docs/03-how-the-ai-works_rev00.md`, `docs/06-investment-decisions_rev01.md`, `docs/08-economy-and-macro_rev01.md`

## Procedure
1. Lead with the answer or recommendation in 1–3 sentences.
2. Use the matching template: investment memo (file 06 §6.5), recommendation memo (file 03), Weekly Macro Brief (file 08 §6.2).
3. Define every technical term the first time (the principal is not a quant).
4. Show dissent and open questions; never hide disagreement.
5. End with sources and the decision needed from the principal.

## Style
Short sentences, tables for comparisons, BRL and USD side by side, dates on every figure.
