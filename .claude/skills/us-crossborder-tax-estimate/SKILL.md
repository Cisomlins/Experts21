# us-crossborder-tax-estimate (S14)

**Used by:** `equities-analyst`, `tax-regulatory-us`, `legal-estate-planner`, `us-tax-advisor-prep`
**Source docs:** `docs/10-regulation-and-tax_rev02.md`

## Procedure
1. Establish US status first: non-resident alien vs. resident (green card or substantial presence test; file 10 §C.2). If unknown, stop and ask.
2. NRA: 30% withholding on US dividends (no US–Brazil income tax treaty); portfolio interest exempt; US estate tax on US-situs assets above US$60,000 (US$13,000 credit).
3. Blockers: offshore holding vs. Irish UCITS ETFs vs. US LLC (risky under COSIT 56/2026) (file 10 §C.1, §B.2).
4. If resident: worldwide income, FBAR/Form 8938, PFIC on Brazilian funds, VGBL treatment open.
5. Flag double-tax issues to the Brazil tax agent.

## Never
Never present the estimate as tax advice.
