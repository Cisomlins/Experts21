## Procedure
1. Triggers: a legal opinion, a tax filing position, a regulated recommendation for someone other than the principal, a contract, a structure change.
2. Prepare a question pack: facts (fictional here), the rule and its date, the options, the specific questions, the documents the professional will need.
3. Name the jurisdiction (BR, US or both) and the type of professional.
4. Mark the answer as **pending professional review** until confirmed.
