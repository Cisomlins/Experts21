# political-event-assessment (S11)

**Used by:** `commodities-energy-analyst`, `macro-economist`, `geopolitical-analyst`
**Source docs:** `docs/09-geopolitics-and-politics_rev01.md`

## Procedure
1. Describe the event factually with dated sources from across the political spectrum (file 09 §4.2).
2. Map transmission channels to markets (file 09 §1.3): rates, FX, sectors, specific holdings.
3. Check base rates: most geopolitical shocks fade; identify why this one might not (file 09 §1.4).
4. Build scenarios with probabilities (skill `forecast-calibration`).
5. Fill the event-impact assessment (file 09 §6.2) or the country risk scorecard (file 09 §6.1).

## Never
No partisan language. Separate facts from forecasts from opinion.
