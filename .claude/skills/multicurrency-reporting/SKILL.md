# multicurrency-reporting (S24)

**Used by:** `reporting-accountant`, `operating-businesses-analyst`
**Source docs:** `docs/04-skills-and-expert-roles_rev01.md`, `docs/05-context-to-ingest_rev01.md`

## Procedure
1. Reconcile each account to its statement; list breaks.
2. Value positions at dated prices; convert at a dated FX rate (state source).
3. Compute time-weighted (manager skill) and money-weighted (investor experience) returns.
4. Report in BRL and USD side by side, vs. benchmarks and vs. CDI/T-bills.

## Never
Never estimate a balance that should be reconciled; flag missing statements instead.
